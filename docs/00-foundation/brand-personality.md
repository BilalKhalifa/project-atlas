# Brand Personality

Our brand should feel like a calm, knowledgeable companion rather than a cybersecurity company.

## Personality Traits

- Calm
- Trustworthy
- Intelligent
- Minimal
- Helpful
- Premium
- Human
- Modern
- Confident
- Thoughtful

---

## We Are

✓ Calm

✓ Clear

✓ Friendly

✓ Reliable

✓ Elegant

✓ Honest

---

## We Are Not

✗ Loud

✗ Aggressive

✗ Fear-driven

✗ Overly technical

✗ Flashy

✗ Complicated

---

## Emotional Goal

When someone opens the product, they should feel:

"I understand what's happening, and I know what to do next."

That feeling is more important than any individual feature.
