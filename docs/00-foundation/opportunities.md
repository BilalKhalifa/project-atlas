# Product Opportunities

## Observation

Existing password managers solve password storage extremely well.

This is not where we want to compete.

---

## Opportunity 1

Become a digital security companion instead of only a password manager.

---

## Opportunity 2

Help users understand what to do after a password compromise.

Example:

- Explain possible risks.
- Recommend recovery steps.
- Prioritize actions.

---

## Opportunity 3

Visualize digital identity.

Instead of showing only passwords, help users understand how their online accounts are connected.

---

## Opportunity 4

Provide simple security guidance.

Translate complex security concepts into understandable actions.

---

## Opportunity 5

Create a product people enjoy opening instead of one they only use when they forget a password.
