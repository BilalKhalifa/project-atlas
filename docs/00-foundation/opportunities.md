# New Product Opportunities

## Observation

Current password managers excel at storing and protecting credentials.

However, they become mostly passive once the vault is organized.

The user is responsible for understanding what security actions to take next.

---

## Opportunity 6 — Security Guidance

Instead of only notifying users about a security issue, actively guide them through recovery.

Example:

- Explain why the issue matters.
- Recommend the order of actions.
- Estimate the recovery time.
- Track recovery progress.

The goal is to reduce anxiety by providing clear, actionable guidance.

---

## Opportunity 7 — Digital Identity Dashboard

Replace a simple password list with a dashboard that helps users understand their overall digital identity.

Potential insights:

- Overall Digital Health Score
- High-priority accounts
- Password reuse
- Old passwords
- Weak passwords
- Missing MFA
- Connected accounts

---

## Opportunity 8 — Human-Centered Security

Translate technical security concepts into language that anyone can understand.

Instead of displaying technical warnings, explain:

- What happened.
- Why it matters.
- What should be done next.
- How much risk remains.

---

## Opportunity 9 — Daily Security Companion

Give users a reason to open the app regularly.

Examples:

- Security tips
- Weekly improvements
- Recommended actions
- Security milestones
- Progress tracking

The product should feel like an active companion rather than passive storage.
