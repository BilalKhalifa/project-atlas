# Product Positioning

## Existing Password Managers

Most password managers are designed around one central goal:

Store passwords securely.

They provide excellent password storage, synchronization, autofill, and password generation.

However, they rarely help users understand or improve their overall digital security.

---

## Our Direction

We are not trying to build a better password vault.

We are building a Digital Identity Platform.

Passwords are only one part of a person's online identity.

Our long-term goal is to help users understand, improve, and confidently manage their digital security through thoughtful design and meaningful guidance.

---

## Positioning Statement

Traditional password managers answer:

"Where are my passwords?"

Our product aims to answer:

"How healthy is my digital identity, and what should I do next?"

---

## Core Difference

Instead of focusing only on password management, the product will gradually evolve into a platform that helps users:

- Organize digital identities
- Understand security risks
- Receive actionable guidance
- Improve long-term security habits
- Feel confident managing their digital life

Our objective is to become a trusted security companion rather than simply a secure vault.
