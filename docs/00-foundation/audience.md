# Target Audience

## MVP Audience

The first version of the product is designed for individuals rather than enterprises.

Our primary audience includes:

- Students
- Developers
- Freelancers
- Creators
- Young professionals

These users often have dozens of online accounts but lack a simple way to understand and improve their overall digital security.

---

## User Characteristics

They:

- Use multiple online services every day.
- Care about privacy but are not security experts.
- Want simple and trustworthy tools.
- Appreciate beautiful, modern user experiences.
- Prefer guidance over technical documentation.

---

## Problems They Face

- Reusing passwords.
- Forgetting where accounts are connected.
- Uncertainty after security incidents.
- Difficulty understanding security terminology.
- Limited visibility into their digital identity.

---

## Goals

Users want to:

- Stay secure with minimal effort.
- Feel confident about their online accounts.
- Understand security risks.
- Improve their digital habits.
- Trust the products they use.

---

## What We Will NOT Target (Initially)

- Large enterprises
- Government organizations
- Security professionals
- Advanced IT administrators

These audiences have different needs and will not be the focus of the first release.
