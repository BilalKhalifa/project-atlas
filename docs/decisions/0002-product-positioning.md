# Decision 0002

## Title

Position the product as a Digital Identity Platform.

## Status

Accepted

## Date

2026-07-16

## Context

During competitor research, we observed that modern password managers such as Bitwarden already provide excellent password storage, synchronization, autofill, passkey support, and security reports.

Competing only on UI or visual design would not create meaningful differentiation.

## Decision

The product will be positioned as a Digital Identity Platform rather than simply another password manager.

Password management will remain a core capability, but the long-term focus will be helping users understand, improve, and manage their overall digital identity through guidance, education, and thoughtful user experience.

## Consequences

Future features should prioritize:

- Guidance over notifications
- Education over complexity
- Digital identity over password storage
- Confidence over fear

Every new feature should reinforce this positioning.
