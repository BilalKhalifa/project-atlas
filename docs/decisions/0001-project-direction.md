# Decision 0001

## Title

Position the product as a Digital Identity Platform rather than a traditional Password Manager.

## Decision

The product will focus on helping users understand and improve their digital security rather than only storing passwords.

## Why

Existing password managers already solve password storage well.

We believe the biggest opportunity is helping users understand, recover from, and improve their overall digital security through guidance, education, and thoughtful user experience.
