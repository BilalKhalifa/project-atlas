# Validated Pain Points (Observed from Community Research)

## 1. Autofill is unreliable

Users frequently report:

- Autofill not appearing.
- Wrong credentials being filled.
- Manual copy-paste still required.

### Opportunity

Make autofill reliable and provide clear guidance when it cannot work.

---

## 2. Password managers provide information but little guidance

Current products notify users about:

- Weak passwords
- Breached passwords
- Reused passwords

However, they rarely guide users through fixing those problems.

### Opportunity

Turn security reports into actionable, step-by-step guidance.

---

## 3. UX friction

Common complaints:

- Too many clicks.
- Confusing organization.
- Outdated interfaces.

### Opportunity

Focus on a calm, intuitive, and efficient experience.

---

# Product Principles (Decided)

These are decisions for Atlas, not differentiators.

- Fully open source.
- Privacy-first.
- No advertisements.
- No vendor lock-in.
- Transparent development.
- Free to use (monetization to be explored later).

These principles establish trust but are **not** our USP because several competitors already share them.

# Product Ideas (Need Validation)

## Automatic Password Update

Scenario:

1. User clicks "Forgot Password" on a website.
2. Password is successfully reset.
3. Atlas detects the credential change.
4. Atlas updates the stored password with minimal user interaction.

### Why it matters

Updating stored passwords after a reset is often inconsistent today and creates friction.

### Status

Idea — requires technical research to determine browser limitations and implementation options.
